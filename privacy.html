Privacy Policy

Effective: July 10, 2026

Deskhand ("we", "us") provides AI-powered lead-response software for home-service businesses. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist or contact us: your name, email, phone number, company name, and anything you include in a message. If you become a customer: business configuration details (services, service area, pricing rules, business hours) and lead/customer message data processed on your behalf to deliver the service.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, provide and improve the service, and send you relevant updates about Deskhand. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your customers' data for anything other than operating your account.

Text messaging & anti-spam compliance

Messages sent by Deskhand on behalf of customers comply with CASL (Canada) and TCPA/CAN-SPAM (US), including opt-out instructions and immediate honoring of STOP requests.

Facebook & Instagram messages

If you connect your Facebook Page or Instagram account, Deskhand receives messages sent to your business through Meta's platforms (Messenger and Instagram Direct), along with the sender's name and profile identifier as provided by Meta. We use this data solely to respond to and manage leads on your behalf — it is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating your account.

Meta message data is retained only as long as needed to provide the service, and is shared only with the service providers listed below as required to deliver it. You (or a message sender) may request deletion of this data at any time by emailing jeffreydommasch@icloud.com; we honor deletion requests promptly.

Third parties

We use trusted processors to run the service (e.g., hosting, SMS delivery, form processing, CRM integrations such as Jobber). They receive only what's needed to perform their function.

Retention & your rights

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing jeffreydommasch@icloud.com.
